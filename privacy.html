Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Who we are

Sacramento Home Offers ("we," "us") is a local home-buying business serving the greater Sacramento area. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit sachomeoffers.com, fill out one of our forms (including Facebook lead forms), or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and details about your property or situation that you choose to share.
  • Information collected automatically: basic browsing data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring site, collected through cookies and similar tools.

How we use your information

  • To evaluate your property and prepare a cash offer or other options you asked about
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your property
  • To improve our website and advertising
  • To comply with legal obligations

Calls and text messages

By submitting your information, you agree that Sacramento Home Offers may call or text you at the number you provided about your property, including by automated dialing systems or prerecorded messages. Consent is not a condition of selling your property or of any purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time, or tell us on a call that you no longer want to be contacted.

We do not sell or share your phone number or text-messaging opt-in with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Cookies and the Meta Pixel

Our website uses the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and similar technologies to measure how our ads perform and to show relevant ads to people who have visited our site. Meta may collect information such as your IP address, browser details, and actions on our page. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook settings and control cookies through your browser settings.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business (for example, CRM, phone, and form tools), who may use it only to provide services to us
  • Title, escrow, and transaction partners if you choose to move forward with a sale
  • Authorities when required by law

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You may also opt out of any sale or sharing of your information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, call or text us at (916) 426-6699. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.

How long we keep information

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, complete any transaction, and meet legal or record-keeping requirements.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Our services are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
